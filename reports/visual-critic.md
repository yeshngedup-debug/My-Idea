Per `reports/SKILL-ASSIGNMENTS.md` the visual role also names `unslop`, `design-taste-frontend` and `apple-design`; those were **not loaded** in this pass, so AI-tell judgments below are grounded in the rendered pages and the orchestrator's recorded unslop findings, not in a fresh reading of those skills.

## What is genuinely good

1. **Real Bhutanese photography carries content on six of seven heroes.** `/for-founders`, `/for-students`, `/contact`, `/faq`, `/how-it-works` and `/about` show gho/kira garments, Thimphu classrooms and stone-wall dzong scenes clearly, each with a Wikimedia credit — e.g. `v2-for-founders-1440.png`, `v2-contact-1440.png`, `v2-about-1440.png`. Only `/public` dims its photo (finding 10).
2. **The closing CTA is disciplined.** One two-line headline (`font-medium`, 44px), one amber button, no pill, badge, glow or gradient text — identical and correctly subordinate on all seven pages (`v2-faq-1440.png`, `v2-public-1440.png`, `v2-for-founders-1440.png`).
3. **Mobile reflow is clean and hierarchy is not weight-only.** Measured at 375px on all seven routes: `document.documentElement.scrollWidth - innerWidth = -15`, zero out-of-viewport elements, zero clipped headings; h1 48px/500, h2 36px/600, h3 16–24px/600, with forest/vermilion colour carrying emphasis alongside size. No pills, gradient text, glow, emoji, exclamation marks or tracked uppercase labels were found in the rendered scoped pages.

## Verification

**Passed (rendered):** dev server answered 200 on all seven routes. Fourteen full-page captures saved with asserted URL + h1: `v2-<route>-1440.png`, `v2-<route>-375.png`. Scroll pass applied before each capture to resolve `whileInView` reveals — the initial full-page capture of `/public` showed empty dark bands, and a scrolled viewport capture (`.playwright-mcp/pub-bhutan.png`) confirmed the same section fully rendered, so **the empty bands were a capture artifact, not a user-facing defect**. Measured 375px overflow, heading line counts and clipping for all seven routes (table above). Confirmed `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"` in source; deferred reduced-motion verdict to `accessibility-critic.md`.

**Not verified:** 320px width and 200% zoom (scope fixed at 375px; accessibility-critic covered 320px); interaction states, keyboard and screen-reader behavior (accessibility-critic owns); contrast ratios (accessibility-critic owns); source edits landing mid-review — `/how-it-works` h1 changed from "From brief to real project." to "Founders post the brief. Students do the work." during this pass (`app/how-it-works/page.tsx:81-82`), so its heading-specific observations are based on the current source and the post-edit DOM measurement only.

**Cross-reference:** no duplication with `reports/copy-critic.md` (copy voice/typography rules), `reports/accessibility-critic.md` (contrast, WCAG, motion), `reports/performance-seo.md` (hero resolution, metadata), `reports/content-integrity.md` (facts, prices, links) or `reports/orchestrator.md` (fix assignment).
