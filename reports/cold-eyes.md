## 1. Direction

- **Product**: Connects Bhutanese students with startup founders through scoped, paid project work, with admin-verified profiles and manually confirmed shortlists and payments.
- **Character**: hand-checked, paper-trail, unhurried.
- **World**: A Thimphu study room beside a founder's desk: printed CVs, stone steps, bank-transfer screenshots, official statistics tables.
- **Moments**: One real local photograph carrying its own light and credit, and the actual shortlist row with its payment-confirmation status timeline.
- **Signature**: Every photograph and figure carries a plain provenance line: photographer and licence under the image, World Bank source under the number, and the explicit note that the photos show places, not members.

## 3. Three things that are genuinely good and must not be broken

1. **Data honesty with visible provenance.** Every national figure is labelled as a national indicator, not a platform result (`data/landing.ts:1-11`, `:30-34`; `app/public/page.tsx:33`, `:250`), and "Why two numbers?" explicitly says the reason for the press-release/API difference is not established (`app/public/page.tsx:271-323`). This is unusually disciplined; do not replace it with a slick stat card or a claim about the product.
2. **The product preview is the real artifact, honestly labelled.** `ShortlistPreviewCompact` reuses the live `StatusTimeline`, `ORDER_STEPS`, badges and row structure, with Tashi Dorji / Pema Choden from the local seed and a "Sample" label (`shortlist-preview.tsx:8-17`, `:89-128`; `sample-shortlist-data.ts:1-7`, `:23-48`). Do not swap it for a hand-built fake dashboard.
3. **Restrained interaction craft.** One easing curve and three durations (`app/globals.css:52-56`), 100ms `scale(0.98)` press feedback (`:103-111`), reduced-transparency and forced-colors fallbacks for the sticky header (`:130-147`), visible focus rings, Phosphor icons at 1.5 stroke, no emoji, no gradient text, no glows. Keep this infrastructure; findings 3 and 6 are exceptions, not a reason to redo it.

## 4. Verdict

**NEEDS WORK.**

Not BLOCK: the direction is product-derived, the copy is specific and honest, and there is no swapped-noun template underneath. Not APPROVE: finding 1 sends founders to the wrong signup role, finding 2 asks the same visitor to perform the same action three times, and finding 3 hides sections from reduced-motion and no-JS visitors. Fix 1-4 first; 5-9 can follow in the same pass.
