# Bhutan Tech Connect — Voice Guide

How we write, and how we differ from the platforms people already know. Upwork sells hustle. Toptal sells exclusivity. Riipen sells institutional warmth. Internshala sells scale. We sell a small, honest bridge: local students, real briefs, people who actually check the work. Our big idea is "back people, not companies."

## Three principles

1. **Plain.** Say what the thing does, using the words already in the product: "pay by bank transfer or mBoB, upload the screenshot, an admin confirms," not "frictionless payments."
2. **Local.** Name Bhutan, Thimphu, college, and the people in the photo; never claim a national scale or a network we do not have.
3. **Human.** A person verifies, ranks, and introduces; we never hide behind an algorithm, an escrow system, or a testimonial we did not earn.

These are constraints, not decoration. Student signup is free and open, founder access is assigned by an administrator, shortlists are admin-curated, payments are manual with no escrow, and every public statistic is a national indicator we did not produce. Write inside those facts.

## Do / Don't

| Don't | Do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Secure, seamless payments" | "Founders cannot confirm their own orders; an admin confirms the payment" | Names the real manual check. |
| "AI-powered matching" | "A manual check, never an algorithm" | This is exactly how verification works on the About page. |
| "Instant, trusted shortlists" | "Ranked shortlist of 3–5 students: name, skills, college, contact, CV links" | Uses our real package text. |
| "Trusted by 10,000+ students" | "Students build a profile and apply; founders set the scope" | No invented counts; real product language. |
| "Guaranteed placements" | "One replacement if a student drops out" | A real entitlement we can honour. |
| "Our impact in numbers: 20.6% youth unemployment" | "20.6% is a World Bank national indicator, not a platform result" | Keeps the source distinct from us. |
| "Meet our happy founders" | "Photographs of places, not members" | We do not show or fabricate member photos. |

## Banned phrases and devices

Never use: em-dashes; "revolutionary"; "seamless"; "AI-powered"; "world-class"; "trusted by" (or any unearned social proof); invented numbers, metrics, or percentages; fake testimonials or quotes; "SECTION 01" style meta-labels; emojis. Use periods, commas, or short sentences instead of em-dashes. Attribute every real figure on the same screen where it appears.

## The "Why two numbers?" honesty rule

The World Bank press release reports **20.6%** youth unemployment; the auto-refreshing API series shows a modeled ILO estimate of **17.1%** for the current year. These are different series, and the reason for the difference is not established here. Keep each number attached to its own source, never blend them into a single "youth unemployment is X%" claim, and never present either as a Bhutan Tech Connect result or outcome. Both are national indicators about Bhutan, not our numbers, and the page already says so.
